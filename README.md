Entrants write one line, at most 160 characters: a software patch note or changelog entry for a bug in real life or human biology, in the terse register of a real release note. Something like "v2.4.1: fixed a bug where the left ear rendered audio 40ms late after a full night of sleep." Every entry is compared two at a time by make.fun's judge against the brief published with the bounty, in private blind rounds, so no one sees a place until results are published. The funniest line takes the top place, and the reward pool is split equally across the top 5 places. Entry is free, and entries stay open for 72 hours.
